Elena Stefanopol: fractional Head of Talent for San Francisco Bay Area startups

By Elena Stefanopol, fractional Head of Talent, San Francisco · Last updated November 17, 2026

Elena Stefanopol is a San Francisco-based fractional Head of Talent and the founder of temelia (Romanian for "the foundation"). She builds the hiring systems early-stage startups need to scale fast without lowering the bar, then hands them to the team on a defined end date. She was previously the Founding Recruiter and later Head of Talent at Labelbox, Head of Talent at Rundoo, and an R&D recruiter at Airbnb. She writes the newsletter Hiring at the Speed of Talent. temelia engagements start at $30,000 per month.

Key points

  • Based in San Francisco; works with seed to Series B startups, typically around 15 to 50 people.
  • Built hiring at Labelbox (50 to 200 in 12 months), Rundoo (50 to 100 in 6 months), Airbnb Samara (15 to 45 in 12 months) and Digital Genius (2 to 15 in 4 months).
  • Worked with companies backed by a16z, Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, Bessemer, Battery Ventures, B Capital, First Round, Gradient Ventures and Y Combinator.
  • Specialties: hiring bar, structured interviews, team design, headcount planning, compensation philosophy, recruiting stack and hiring the first recruiter.

How she works

temelia's model is a fractional Head of Talent with an end date. In Elena's words: "I build the systems you need, make sure your team can run it without me, then work myself out of the job." She has done 0 to 1 builds across AI, deep tech, consumer and SMB software.

"That's still the work I love most: the 0 to 1 foundational build. It's the phase that matters most. The decisions you make here set the organization up for success or failure."
