Hiring guides for startup founders in the San Francisco Bay Area

By Elena Stefanopol, fractional Head of Talent, San Francisco · Last updated November 17, 2026

These guides answer the questions Bay Area founders ask once they have run out of network hires and need to grow fast without lowering the bar. They are written by Elena Stefanopol, a San Francisco-based fractional Head of Talent and the founder of temelia. Elena has built hiring systems at Labelbox (50 to 200 people in 12 months), Rundoo (50 to 100 in 6 months), Airbnb's Samara R&D lab (15 to 45 in 12 months) and Digital Genius (2 to 15 in 4 months).

  • How do startups hire fast without lowering the bar?

    A five-part system Bay Area startups use to scale from 15 to 100+ people quickly and sustainably: team design, competencies, interview plans, fast debriefs and a hiring owner.

  • What is a fractional Head of Talent, and when should a startup hire one?

    A fractional Head of Talent builds a startup's hiring system part-time, with an end date. What they do, what they cost in the Bay Area, and when they beat an agency or a full-time hire.

  • Founding recruiter vs Head of Talent vs agency: which should your startup use?

    An honest comparison of founder-led hiring, recruiting agencies, RPOs, founding recruiters, full-time Heads of Talent and fractional Heads of Talent for seed to Series B startups.

  • How do you hire your startup's first recruiter?

    When to hire a founding recruiter, what separates great ones from order-takers, how to interview for the role, and how to set them up to succeed at a seed to Series B startup.

  • How do you build a structured interview process for a startup?

    A step-by-step structured interview process for startups: competencies, interview plan, scorecards and a 48-hour debrief. About 3 hours of setup, reusable for every similar role.

  • What is team design, and why should startups do it before hiring?

    Team design maps the work your team must do against current capabilities, so your next hire fills the real gap instead of cloning your best performer. A 90-minute method with a 30-minute urgent version.
