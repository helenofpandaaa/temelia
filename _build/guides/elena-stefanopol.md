## How she works

temelia's model is a **fractional Head of Talent with an end date**. In Elena's words: "I build the systems you need, make sure your team can run it without me, then work myself out of the job." She has done 0 to 1 builds across AI, deep tech, consumer and SMB software.

> "That's still the work I love most: the 0 to 1 foundational build. It's the phase that matters most. The decisions you make here set the organization up for success or failure."
>
> Elena Stefanopol, [She Builds With AI, Teams of Tomorrow interview](https://shebuildswithai.substack.com/p/ai-didnt-break-hiring-it-made-the)
